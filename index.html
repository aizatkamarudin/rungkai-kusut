Frequently asked.

How much does a dashboard or digitalisation project cost?
Every project starts with a free 45-minute data health check. After that we give a fixed-price quote, and most SMEs start with one small project, such as one dashboard or one digitalised process, before expanding.
Can you digitalise our paper forms and WhatsApp approvals?
Yes. We turn paper and Excel processes such as claims, leave, orders and inspections into digital forms with automatic approval workflows that staff can use on their phones.
Can you connect our POS, accounting and other systems?
Yes. We build API integrations between payment, POS, accounting, CRM, online store, Google Workspace and Microsoft 365, so data moves automatically instead of being retyped.
Do you build mobile apps and IoT solutions?
Yes. We build cross-platform iOS and Android apps, and integrate IoT devices and sensors into dashboards and alerts, as in our parking, enforcement and water-monitoring projects.
Is our data safe?
We design security, access control and data privacy in line with Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) from day one, and you keep ownership of your data and systems.
Do you only serve companies in Kuala Lumpur?
We are based in Kuala Lumpur and meet clients in Klang Valley in person. We also work online with SMEs across Malaysia.
Boleh berurusan dalam Bahasa Malaysia?
Boleh. Kami berkhidmat dalam Bahasa Malaysia dan English, termasuk latihan dan dokumentasi untuk pasukan anda.
Can my project use a government digitalisation grant?
We can help you scope a grant-ready digitalisation project and prepare the technical details. Eligibility and approval are decided by the grant agency or bank.
