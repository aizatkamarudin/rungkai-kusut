Kenapa ia penting

PDPA got stricter.

Malaysia's Personal Data Protection (Amendment) Act 2024 added biometric data to sensitive personal data and raised the maximum fine to RM1 million from April 2025. Since June 2025, data controllers must notify the Commissioner of data breaches, and many must appoint a data protection officer.
